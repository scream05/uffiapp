Our Community Rules

Rund um Uffni is meant to be a friendly place for everyone in Uffenheim. Here are the most important rules, short and sweet. The Terms of Use are binding.

  1. Use your real name Sign up with your real first and last name, one person per account. That way everyone knows who they're talking to.
  2. Stay friendly Criticism of the matter at hand is welcome, personal attacks are not. No insults, no threats, no bullying.
  3. No hate speech No one may be demeaned on the basis of origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or age.
  4. Stick to the truth No rumors about others, no deliberately false claims, and no posing under someone else's name.
  5. Respect privacy No data, photos or messages of others without their consent – especially no pictures of children. Only upload what you have the rights to.
  6. Advertise in moderation Notices about your own offers in Uffenheim – club, flea market, farm shop – are fine in the appropriate section. Spam, mass advertising and chain letters are not. Businesses introduce themselves in the business directory.
  7. Keep politics factual Topics concerning Uffenheim may and should be discussed. Election advertising from parties and voter groups does not belong here – this also applies to us as the operator.
  8. Take the right route Report damage via the Problem Reporter, dates via the club's page or as a poster via WhatsApp, and suggestions for the platform under Ideas & Suggestions.

If something doesn't look right

Do you see a post or comment that breaks these rules? Report it via the menu on the post ("Report") or by e-mail to smartcity@buergerliste-uffenheim.de.

We look at every report. Violations are hidden or deleted, and we usually tell you why. In cases of repeated or serious violations, we may issue warnings and block accounts. If you don't agree with a decision, write to us – we will review it again.

Rund um Uffni is a volunteer citizens' platform of the Bürgerliste Uffenheim e. V. – not an offering of the town of Uffenheim.
