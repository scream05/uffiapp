Accessibility Statement

Rund um Uffni is a citizen platform of the Bürgerliste Uffenheim e. V. Everyone should be able to use it, including people with disabilities and older people. This means that the pages can be operated with a keyboard, screen reader, strong magnification and on a phone.

We are not a public body or an authority. The legal obligations for websites of public bodies (Bavarian Disability Equality Act, BayBITV) therefore do not apply to us. Nevertheless, we voluntarily follow the same rules: the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

How accessible is the platform?

The platform is partially accessible. Most pages meet the requirements. Some content and features still have barriers, which we list below.

What we have done

  • Sufficient contrast for text, input fields and controls, including in dark mode
  • Visible focus when operating with the keyboard and a "Skip to content" link at the top of the page
  • Links are underlined and not recognizable by colour alone
  • Buttons and icons have names for screen readers, and pages have a clear structure with headings and regions
  • Buttons are large enough to tap, and pages can be zoomed on a phone
  • The map of problem reports is also available as a list, and you can also enter the location of a report by address
  • Images in posts can receive an image description: whoever writes a post adds it via the menu on the post ("Image descriptions"). Images from uffenheim.de, event posters and the Mitteilungsblatt automatically receive a short description
  • You can add appointments to your own calendar as a calendar subscription
  • The security check during sign-up (ALTCHA) works without picture puzzles
  • The platform is available in six languages

What is not yet accessible

  • Images in posts: Newly uploaded images often have no image description until someone adds one. Then the screen reader reads out the file name or "image without description". The descriptions of images that existed until October 2026 were created by an AI; they may contain errors.
  • Mitteilungsblatt and bylaws: The PDF files come from the City of Uffenheim and are often scanned. Screen readers can read them only to a limited extent or not at all. We have no influence over the content.
  • Imported content: We automatically import news and appointments from www.uffenheim.de. Barriers from the source are retained in the process, for example missing image descriptions.
  • Posts from users: For texts, images, videos and files that others upload, we cannot guarantee that they are accessible.
  • PDF forms created by the Uffi assistant: These PDFs are not prepared for screen readers.
  • Calendar in month view and maps (problem reports, business directory): They can only be used to a limited extent with a screen reader. You will find the same information in the lists or in the individual entries.
  • Easy language and sign language are not yet offered.

How we tested it

We tested the platform ourselves. The automated check was carried out with the axe-core tool on a computer and at phone size, without signing in and with a member account (home page, profile, settings, messages, Uffi, problem reports, suggestions, vouchers, directories). In addition, we carried out spot checks with the keyboard. A test with screen readers by affected people is still pending. The last check was on 29 October 2026. An audit by an independent testing body has not taken place so far.
