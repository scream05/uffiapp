Accessibility Statement

Rund um Uffni is a citizens' platform of the Bürgerliste Uffenheim e. V. Everyone should be able to use it, including people with disabilities and older people. This means that the pages can be operated with a keyboard, screen reader, strong magnification and on a phone.

We are not a public body or an authority. The legal obligations for websites of public bodies (Bavarian Disability Equality Act, BayBITV) therefore do not apply to us. Nevertheless, we voluntarily follow the same rules: the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

How accessible is the platform?

The platform is partially accessible. Most pages meet the requirements. Some content and features still have barriers, which we list below.

What we have done about it

  • Sufficient contrast for text, input fields and controls, including in dark mode
  • Visible focus marking when operating with the keyboard and a "Skip to content" link at the top of the page
  • Links are underlined and not recognizable by colour alone
  • Buttons and icons have names for screen readers, and pages have a clear structure with headings and regions
  • Buttons are large enough to tap, and pages can be zoomed on your phone
  • The map of problem reports is also available as a list, and you can also enter the location of a report by address
  • You can add events to your own calendar as a calendar subscription
  • The security check when logging in (ALTCHA) works without picture puzzles
  • The platform is available in six languages

What is not yet accessible

  • Images in posts: Uploaded images usually have no image description, because HumHub, the software behind the platform, does not provide an input field for this. We are working on a solution.
  • Official bulletin and statutes: The PDF files come from the City of Uffenheim and are often scanned. Screen readers can read them only to a limited extent or not at all. We have no influence over the content.
  • Imported content: We automatically import news and events from www.uffenheim.de. Barriers from the source are retained in the process, for example missing image descriptions.
  • Posts from users: We cannot guarantee that texts, images, videos and files uploaded by others are accessible.
  • PDF forms created by the Uffi assistant: These PDFs are not prepared for screen readers.
  • Calendar in month view and maps (problem reports, business directory): They can only be used to a limited extent with a screen reader. You will find the same information in the lists or in the individual entries.
  • Areas for logged-in members (for example writing posts, settings, redeeming vouchers) have not yet been fully checked.
  • Easy language and sign language are not yet offered.

How we tested it

We checked the platform ourselves. The automated check was carried out with the axe-core tool on a computer and at phone size, supplemented by spot checks using the keyboard. The last check was on 29 October 2026. There has been no review by an independent testing body so far.
