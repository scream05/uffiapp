How to use Rund um Uffni

The best way to use Rund um Uffni on your phone is as a web app: an icon on your home screen that opens like an app – without the App Store, free of charge and always up to date. On PC and tablet it works in any browser. You can even read, ask Uffi and report problems without an account.

As an app on your phone (recommended)

  1. Open the page. Open community.rundumuffni.de on an iPhone in Safari, or on an Android phone in Chrome.
  2. Add it to your home screen. iPhone and iPad: tap the Share button , then "Add to Home Screen". Android: tap the menu at the top right, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Open the new "Rund um Uffni" icon and log in or register there. How to do that is described further below.
  4. Allow notifications when the page asks – or turn them on under Account → Notifications. Then reminders about waste collection and replies to your posts will work.

The web app opens without an address bar, receives push notifications and shows the number of your new notifications and messages on the icon. The number increases with each push notification and updates as soon as you open the web app. On iPhone this requires iOS 16.4 or later.

On PC and tablet

Open community.rundumuffni.de in your browser – nothing more is needed. On a tablet you can add the page to your home screen just like on a phone.

Create an account

  1. Tap "Log in", enter your e-mail address when registering and tap "Register".
  2. You will receive an e-mail with a link. Tap it. No e-mail? Check your spam folder.
  3. Choose your password and enter your real first and last name.
  4. Done – you're in right away. A short tour shows you the most important things.

Setting up notifications

You can see new notifications via the bell. What you receive – in the bell, as a push notification on your phone or by e-mail – is set under Account → Notifications.

Not receiving push notifications? Check in your phone's settings whether notifications are allowed for "Rund um Uffni". In a normal browser tab on a phone, push often doesn't work – open Rund um Uffni via the icon on your home screen instead.

First steps

  • Waste collection reminder Choose Kernstadt or a district and your bins – we'll remind you the evening before or in the morning. Set up
  • Ask Uffi The round button at the bottom right opens Uffi, our AI assistant. It knows opening hours, forms, appointments and much more.
  • Report a problem Pothole or broken street lamp? Take a photo, set the location on the map, and send. Report a problem

  • Language Rund um Uffni is also available in English, Ukrainian, Turkish, Russian and Arabic. When logged in under Account → Settings, without an account at the very bottom of the page.

Help with problems

I have forgotten my password.
Via Forgot password we will send you a link to reset it.
The confirmation e-mail doesn't arrive.
Check your spam folder and wait a few minutes. If that doesn't help, write to us.
I have installed the app "HumHub".
We currently don't recommend the app "HumHub", because it doesn't run reliably with us yet – for example, after a break it often only shows a blue area. Instead, set up the web app and log in there with your existing account. After that you can delete the app "HumHub", otherwise push notifications will arrive twice.
I would like to delete my account.
You can do this at any time under Account → Delete account.
I have another question.
Ask Uffi or write to smartcity@buergerliste-uffenheim.de. We welcome ideas for new features under Ideas & Suggestions.
