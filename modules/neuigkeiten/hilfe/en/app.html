How to use Rund um Uffni

Rund um Uffni runs in the free app "HumHub" and in any browser. Reading, asking Uffi and reporting problems even works without an account.

In the app (recommended)

  1. Download the "HumHub" app. Search for "HumHub" in the App Store (iPhone, iPad) or on Google Play (Android). The app is free.
  2. Enter the address. When you start the app for the first time, it asks for the address. Type in: community.rundumuffni.de
  3. Log in or register. You'll find out how further down.
  4. Allow notifications when the app asks. Then you'll get reminders about waste collection and replies to your posts.

"HumHub" is the app, "Rund um Uffni" is the community within it. The app currently doesn't show a number on the app icon – you'll see new notifications via the bell.

In the browser – also as an icon on the home screen

Open community.rundumuffni.de on your PC, tablet or phone. On your phone you can add the page to your home screen like an app:

  • iPhone and iPad (Safari): Tap the Share button , then "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android (Chrome): Tap the menu at the top right, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

Create an account

  1. Tap "Log in", enter your e-mail address when registering and tap "Register".
  2. You'll get an e-mail with a link. Tap it. No e-mail? Check your spam folder.
  3. Choose your password and enter your real first and last name.
  4. Done – you're in straight away. A short tour shows you the most important things.

Set up notifications

You'll see new notifications via the bell. You set what you get – in the bell, as a push notification on your phone or by e-mail – under Account → Notifications.

Not receiving push notifications? Check the settings on your phone to see whether notifications are allowed for the "HumHub" app. Push works most reliably in the app.

First steps

  • Waste collection reminder Choose Kernstadt or a district and your bins – we'll remind you the evening before or in the morning. Set up
  • Ask Uffi The round button at the bottom right opens Uffi, our AI assistant. It knows opening hours, forms, appointments and much more.
  • Report a problem Pothole or broken streetlight? Take a photo, set the location on the map, and send. Report a problem
  • My overview In the profile menu you choose which areas' posts appear on your home page. Adjust
  • Language Rund um Uffni is also available in English, Ukrainian, Turkish, Russian and Arabic. When logged in, under Account → Settings; without an account, at the very bottom of the page.
  • Tour You can view the welcome tour again at any time.

Help with problems

I've forgotten my password.
Via Forgot password we'll send you a link to reset it.
The confirmation e-mail isn't arriving.
Check your spam folder and wait a few minutes. If that doesn't help, write to us.
The app can't find the page.
Enter the address exactly like this: community.rundumuffni.de – without "www".
I want to delete my account.
You can do this at any time under Account → Delete account.
I have a different question.
Ask Uffi or write to smartcity@buergerliste-uffenheim.de. We welcome ideas for new features under Ideas & Suggestions.
