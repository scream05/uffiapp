How to use Rund um Uffni

Rund um Uffni runs in the free app "HumHub" and in any browser. You can read, ask Uffi and report problems even without an account.

In the app (recommended)

  1. Download the "HumHub" app. Search for "HumHub" in the App Store (iPhone, iPad) or on Google Play (Android). The app is free.
  2. Enter the address. When you first start the app, it asks for the address. Type in: community.rundumuffni.de
  3. Log in or register. You'll find out how this works further down.
  4. Allow notifications when the app asks. This way you'll get bin collection reminders and replies to your posts.

"HumHub" is the app, "Rund um Uffni" is the community inside it. The app currently doesn't show a number on the app icon – you'll see new notifications via the bell.

In the browser – also as an icon on your home screen

Open community.rundumuffni.de on your PC, tablet or phone. On your phone, you can add the site to your home screen like an app:

  • iPhone and iPad (Safari): Tap the Share button , then "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android (Chrome): Tap the menu at the top right, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

Create an account

  1. Tap "Log in", enter your e-mail address when registering and tap "Register".
  2. You'll get an e-mail with a link. Tap it. No e-mail? Check your spam folder.
  3. Set your password and enter your real first and last name.
  4. Done – you're in straight away. A short tour shows you the most important things.

Set up notifications

You'll see new notifications via the bell. You can set what you receive – in the bell, as a push notification on your phone, or by e-mail – under Account → Notifications.

Not getting push notifications? Check the settings on your phone to see whether notifications are allowed for the "HumHub" app. Push works most reliably in the app.

Getting started

  • Bin collection reminder Choose Kernstadt or a district and your bins – we'll remind you the evening before or in the morning. Set up
  • Ask Uffi The round button at the bottom right opens Uffi, our AI assistant. It knows opening hours, forms, appointments and much more.
  • Report a problem Pothole or broken street lamp? Take a photo, set the location on the map, and send. Report a problem
  • My overview In the profile menu you choose which areas' posts appear on your home page. Set up
  • Language Rund um Uffni is also available in English, Ukrainian, Turkish, Russian and Arabic. When logged in, go to Account → Settings; without an account, scroll all the way down the page.
  • Tour You can view the welcome tour again at any time here.

Help with problems

I've forgotten my password.
Via Forgot password we'll send you a link to reset it.
The confirmation e-mail isn't arriving.
Check your spam folder and wait a few minutes. If that doesn't help, write to us.
The app can't find the site.
Enter the address exactly as follows: community.rundumuffni.de – without "www".
After a break, the app only shows a blue background.
This is a known bug in the HumHub app when the phone has put it to sleep in the background. Close the app completely (swipe it away in the overview of open apps) and open it again – then everything is back.
I want to delete my account.
You can do this at any time under Account → Delete account.
I have another question.
Ask Uffi or write to smartcity@buergerliste-uffenheim.de. We welcome ideas for new features under Ideas & Suggestions.
