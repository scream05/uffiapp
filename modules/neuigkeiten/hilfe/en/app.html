How to use Rund um Uffni

Rund um Uffni runs in the free app "HumHub" and in any browser. Reading, asking Uffi and reporting problems even works without an account.

In the app (recommended)

  1. Download the app "HumHub". Search for "HumHub" in the App Store (iPhone, iPad) or on Google Play (Android). The app is free.
  2. Enter the address. When you first open the app, it asks for the address. Type in: community.rundumuffni.de
  3. Log in or register. You'll find out how this works further down.
  4. Allow notifications when the app asks. That way you'll get reminders for waste collection and replies to your posts.

"HumHub" is the app, "Rund um Uffni" is the community inside it. A number on the app icon shows you how many new notifications and messages are waiting for you.

In the browser – also as an icon on your home screen

Open community.rundumuffni.de on your PC, tablet or phone. On your phone you can add the page to your home screen like an app:

  • iPhone and iPad (Safari): Tap the Share button , then "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android (Chrome): Tap the menu at the top right, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

This turns Rund um Uffni into a web app: it opens without an address bar, receives push notifications and shows the number of your new notifications and messages on the icon. To do this, open it via the icon, log in and allow notifications when the page asks – or turn them on under Account → Notifications. On an iPhone this works from iOS 16.4 onwards.

The number increases with every push notification and updates as soon as you open the web app.

Creating an account

  1. Tap "Log in", enter your e-mail address when registering and tap "Register".
  2. You'll receive an e-mail with a link. Tap it. No e-mail? Check your spam folder.
  3. Choose your password and enter your real first and last name.
  4. Done – you're in straight away. A short tour shows you the most important things.

Setting up notifications

You'll see new notifications via the bell. What you receive – in the bell, as a push notification on your phone or by e-mail – is set under Account → Notifications.

Not receiving push notifications? Check the settings on your phone to see whether notifications are allowed for the app "HumHub" or for the "Rund um Uffni" icon (web app). Push often doesn't work in a normal browser tab on a phone – use the app or the web app instead.

Getting started

  • Waste collection reminder Choose Kernstadt or a district and your bins – we'll remind you the evening before or in the morning. Set up
  • Ask Uffi The round button at the bottom right opens Uffi, our AI assistant. It knows opening hours, forms, appointments and much more.
  • Report a problem Pothole or broken street lamp? Take a photo, place the location on the map, send. Report a problem
  • My overview In the profile menu you choose which areas' posts appear on your start page. Adjust
  • Language Rund um Uffni is also available in English, Ukrainian, Turkish, Russian and Arabic. When logged in, go to Account → Settings; without an account, scroll to the bottom of the page.
  • Tour You can view the welcome tour again at any time.

Help with problems

I've forgotten my password.
Via Forgot password we'll send you a link to reset it.
The confirmation e-mail doesn't arrive.
Check your spam folder and wait a few minutes. If that doesn't help, write to us.
The app can't find the site.
Enter the address exactly like this: community.rundumuffni.de – without "www".
After a break, the app only shows a blue area.
This is a bug in the app "HumHub": the phone pauses it in the background, and it doesn't reload the page afterwards. Until a new app version fixes this, you can use Rund um Uffni as an icon on your home screen – there the browser reloads the page by itself, and push notifications along with the number on the icon also still arrive.
I want to delete my account.
You can do this at any time under Account → Delete account.
I have a different question.
Ask Uffi or write to smartcity@buergerliste-uffenheim.de. We welcome ideas for new features under Ideas & Suggestions.
